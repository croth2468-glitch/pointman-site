Privacy and contact

Last updated November 10, 2026

PointMan is an AI career advisor app for iPhone. This page explains what information we collect, why, who helps us process it, and how you can delete it. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is unclear, contact us.

Contact us

Email info@pointman-coaching.com, or tap Send feedback in the Profile tab of the app. We usually reply within a few days.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password (stored securely; we never see the password itself).
  • What you tell PointMan: your onboarding answers, such as school or work status, field of study, interests, skills, industries, goals, and values.
  • Your resume, if you upload one.
  • Your chats with the PointMan advisor, including files and photos you attach, and the notes PointMan keeps about you so it remembers your goals between conversations.
  • Your guides and the checklist items you complete or add.
  • Survey answers and feedback you choose to send us.
  • Waitlist email, if you join the waitlist on this website.

We do not collect your location or contacts, we do not use advertising trackers in the app, and we do not sell your information.

How we use it

  • To give you career recommendations, guides, and chat answers that fit you.
  • To keep your account working across sign-ins and devices.
  • To limit free beta usage and to improve PointMan based on survey answers and feedback.
  • To email you about your account (for example, confirming your email address) and, if you joined the waitlist, to tell you when PointMan is available.

Who helps us

We share information only with the services that run PointMan, and only what they need:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, and file storage.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model. Your profile, chat messages, attachments, and resume text are sent to it to write answers and guides. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Apple delivers the app through the App Store and TestFlight.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the Profile tab and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, resume, guides, and chats. Feedback notes you sent are kept without your name or account attached. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls so other people can't read it. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put passwords or financial details in your chats.

Children

PointMan is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For big changes, we will also let you know in the app.
