The problem

Your career is the biggest decision you'll make. Most people make it alone.

Whether you're picking a major, hunting for your first job or thinking about a change, each choice shapes the next. The help on offer wasn't built for a decision that big.

01 · Quizzes

They ask what you want.

"Which industry interests you?" is the question you came here to answer, not one you can answer on day one.

02 · One-off advising

One meeting, then nothing.

A single appointment, then you're on your own until the next deadline.

03 · Forgotten context

Every time, from scratch.

Nobody remembers what you said last time, so advice never builds on itself.

So people guess, switch paths, and stay unsure. PointMan starts with what you already know about yourself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

I have no idea what I want to do. Is this still for me?

Yes, that's who it's built for. You never have to name an industry or a dream job. PointMan works from what you've liked, what you're good at and what you'd rather avoid.

Who is PointMan for?

Anyone figuring out what's next: people choosing what to study, looking for a first job, or ready for a change.

Does it remember me?

Yes. PointMan keeps your profile and conversation history so advice builds on what you've already shared.

What is a guide?

A short adaptive question session that turns into a personal plan with concrete steps. You can make a new one any time.

When can I try it?

PointMan is in a small beta on iPhone right now. Join the waitlist and we'll send you an invite as we open more spots.

How much does it cost?

It's free during the beta.

What happens to my information?

Your profile and chats are used only to give you better career advice. We don't sell your data, and you can delete your account from inside the app.
